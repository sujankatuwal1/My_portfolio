01 / Restaurant management platform

Restra

A comprehensive multi-tenant restaurant management platform designed to streamline daily restaurant operations. Restra includes guest-facing digital menus and ordering, table sessions, reservations, POS workflows, kitchen ticket management, order tracking, staff operations, inventory control, purchasing, supplier management, loyalty programs, analytics, reporting, and super-admin tenant management. The platform supports real-time updates, QR-based table ordering, role-based access control, offline-ready staff workflows, data imports, invoice management, and multi-outlet restaurant administration.
